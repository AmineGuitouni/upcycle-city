Synthesised for this project with ffmpeg (no third-party source, free to use like the rest of the game):
`SFX/Intro/ambience_wind_loop.wav` (filtered brown noise with slow gusts), `heartbeat.wav` (enveloped low sines),
`sting.wav` (pitch-dropping boom with a noise tail), `ambience_underwater_loop.wav` (low-passed noise and a 62 Hz hum).

With ffmpeg, the wave files were converted from 24-bit FLAC to 16-bit WAV and the gull files were downmixed to mono; they are otherwise unchanged.
